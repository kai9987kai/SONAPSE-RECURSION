# Scientific limitations

Eight independent seeds quantify variability within the chosen generators. Percentile bootstrap intervals summarize per-metric means and may be narrow when a task reaches its deterministic ceiling. Confirmatory inference would require paired effect intervals, adequate replication, a held-out protocol and correction for multiple outcomes.

Continuous rate dynamics are smooth and contractive by design. Discontinuous resets, collisions and changing weights expose large errors. The bounded readout can spend almost an entire rollout at saturation. Reporting one-step error alone would conceal this failure. The graph-conditioned experiment supplies a known aggregation rule, and a learned neural emulator is judged only against its particular discrete reference.

Host bank coordinates count represented state. Weights, scratch activations, channels, teacher generation and training also consume physical resources. No biological energy calibration, inferred synaptic physiology, GPU timing, neural tissue, human data or independently demonstrated cognitive enhancement is part of this release. Biological plausibility cannot be inferred from model names.
