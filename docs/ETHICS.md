# Ethics and interpretation

All environments, neurons, communication channels and agents are mathematical simulations. Survival means reaching an episode horizon under a specified scoring rule. Energy means an activity proxy unless a physical unit and measuring device are explicitly identified.

No subject data, interventions or medical decisions enter the software. The virtual BCI is a synthetic software channel. Any proposed human, clinical or organoid work would require an independent research design, appropriate scientific expertise and applicable oversight. Software results here cannot establish feasibility or safety for such work.
